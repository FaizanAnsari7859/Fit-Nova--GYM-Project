About FitNova

A private training floor built for people who take progress seriously.

FitNova is a performance gym for strength, conditioning, and recovery. The floor is stocked with commercial equipment, the coaching is personal, and every program starts with how you move today.

Whether you are starting your first cycle or preparing for competition, you train in a room that respects effort and keeps the details sharp.
